UTF-8 Character Encoding

This webpage demonstrates the use of UTF-8 character encoding. UTF-8 allows a webpage to display characters from different languages correctly.

English: Hello, welcome to my webpage.
Hindi: नमस्ते, मेरी वेबसाइट पर आपका स्वागत है।
Japanese: こんにちは、私のウェブページへようこそ。

UTF-8 supports a wide range of characters, symbols, and languages. The text above verifies that English, Hindi, and Japanese characters are displayed correctly.

Thank you for visiting this UTF-8 encoding demonstration page.
